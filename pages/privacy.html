Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PreTrack. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data when using our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

When you use PreTrack, we may collect information including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google account information (when signing in with Google)
  • Study goals
  • Subjects
  • Task completion history
  • Progress statistics
  • Device information
  • Browser type
  • Cookies and analytics data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized study tracking.
  • Maintain your account.
  • Synchronize your study progress.
  • Improve our services.
  • Analyze website performance.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Protect against abuse and fraud.

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, we receive basic profile information authorized by your Google account, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, or other Google services without your explicit permission.

5. Firebase Services

PreTrack uses Google Firebase services including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to securely manage user accounts and synchronize study data. Your information is stored using industry-standard security practices provided by Google Cloud infrastructure.

6. Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember preferences, measure website performance, and enhance security. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function correctly.

7. Third-Party Services

PreTrack may use trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google Analytics (if enabled)
  • Google AdSense (when available)
  • EmailJS (for contact forms)
  • Render (website hosting)

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies before using related features.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns.

10. Children's Privacy

PreTrack is intended for students and learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements or business practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
