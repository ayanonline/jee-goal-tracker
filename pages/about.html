About PreTrack

Learn how PreTrack was created to support students in their JEE preparation by helping them track daily goals and stay consistent.

Our Mission

At PreTrack, our mission is to simplify your JEE preparation by helping you stay organized, consistent, and motivated. We understand the importance of daily discipline and structured tracking, especially when aiming for top engineering colleges.

Founded in 2025, PreTrack evolved from a personal tool to a public platform designed to help students monitor their daily goals, track progress, and build better habits during their JEE journey.

About the Founder

Hi, I'm Ayan Sarkar, the creator of PreTrack. As a JEE aspirant and tech enthusiast, I built this tool to stay accountable in my own preparation. Soon, I realized it could help others too. PreTrack is my way of contributing to the student community striving for success in one of the toughest exams in India.
